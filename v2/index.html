About IAML

About Us

The central aim of the International Association for Mongolic Linguistics (IAML), founded in August 2023, is to promote the study of the Mongolic languages in all their varieties, both historical and modern. IAML is the first global umbrella organization which provides a central contact point for all those who are working on Mongolic languages.

Principal Objectives

actively contributing to the development of all subject areas of Mongolic linguistics

providing an international platform for discussing and comparing results of fundamental research in the field of Mongolic linguistics;

establishing collaborative agreements and working relations with any other professional organizations with related interests;

disseminating knowledge about various aspects of the Mongolic languages;

supporting Mongolic linguistics.

Public Activities

01

Biennial Conference

holding a biennial IAML conference, also known as the International Conference on Mongolic Linguistics (ML), serving as a comprehensive forum for exchange of novel knowledge and experience in investigating various aspects of Mongolic languages;

02

Newsletter

issuing a regular newsletter where information about various events in the field of Mongolic linguistics and the activities of the association is provided;

03

Research Publications

promoting the dissemination of research results in the field of Mongolic linguistics in the form of conference materials and – in the future – an academic journal;

04

Fieldwork

encouraging linguistic fieldwork for the documentation of modern Mongolic language varieties;

05

Grants & Scholarships

awarding grants and scholarships for students and young scholars working on Mongolic languages;

06

Fellowships

providing fellowships for participating in IAML conferences for academics;

07

Summer & Winter Schools

organizing Summer Schools/Winter Schools in Mongolic linguistics for Mongolian and international university students and teachers in Mongolia.

Committees

Governing Bodies

IAML's work is guided by dedicated committees of scholars from around the world.

Executive Board

The Executive Board oversees the strategic direction and governance of IAML.

Scientific Committee

Sets research priorities and reviews submissions for IAML conferences and publications.

Editorial Board

Manages the IAML newsletter and oversees forthcoming academic publications.

Membership Committee

Welcomes new members and supports the IAML community of scholars and educators.
