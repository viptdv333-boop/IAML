Mongolist

Subscribe by email to what matters to you. Registration and log in are required.

This international mailing list aims to connect linguists working on Mongolic languages, as well as anyone with an interest in the field. It serves as a platform for sharing announcements, news, and upcoming events, as well as for academic discussions, inquiries, and scholarly recommendations. We warmly welcome you to join the list and invite your colleagues. Please feel free to suggest any relevant topics or issues you would like to see addressed.

Code of Conduct: Subscribers are expected to respect the opinions of others and maintain a professional, constructive tone during all discussions and debates.

How to Subscribe

To subscribe, please send an email to <xxx@xxx> with the following command in the body of the message:

How to Unsubscribe

If you wish to leave this mailing list, you can unsubscribe at any time by sending an email to the same address with the following command in the body of the message:

unsubscribe mongolist
