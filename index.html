About IAML

About Us

The International Association for Mongolic Linguistics (IAML) was founded on August 16, 2023, in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, by a group of scholars specializing in Mongolic language studies. The mission of the Association is to promote interaction and collaboration among researchers and students across all branches of Mongolic linguistics, encompassing diverse theoretical orientations and research focuses. The working languages of the Association are Mongolian and English.

The Association operates on a two-year cycle, organizing a biennial conference, called the International Conference on Mongolic Linguistics (ML). This conference series was initiated by Pavel Rykin (now at the School of Mongolian Studies, Inner Mongolia University), who organized the international conference “Mongolic Languages: History and Present” in October 2013 at the Institute for Linguistic Studies, Russian Academy of Sciences (St. Petersburg, Russia)—an event now recognized as the First International Conference on Mongolic Linguistics. The ML serves as a comprehensive forum for exchanging novel knowledge and experience in investigating various aspects of Mongolic languages. Conference venues typically alternate between different universities and research institutes in Asia and Europe, though other regions may also be considered. The next conference will be hosted in 2027 by Inner Mongolia University, China, with Prof. Wu Yingzhe as the head of the Organizing Committee. The Biennial General Meeting is always held during each ML conference.

IAML also aims to disseminate information about Mongolic linguistics worldwide. For instance, the present website provides links to existing online resources on Mongolic languages (corpora, data banks, dictionaries, textbooks, and monographs), a regularly updated mailing list of researchers and students, and a newsletter covering new publications, conferences, and projects, intended for both specialists and the general public.

Committees

Governing Bodies

IAML's work is guided by dedicated committees of scholars from around the world.

Executive Board

The Executive Board oversees the strategic direction and governance of IAML.

Scientific Committee

Sets research priorities and reviews submissions for IAML conferences and publications.

Editorial Board

Manages the IAML newsletter and oversees forthcoming academic publications.

Membership Committee

Welcomes new members and supports the IAML community of scholars and educators.
