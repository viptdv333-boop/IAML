About IAML

Bridging Cultures Through Linguistic Scholarship

Founded in 2026, IAML brings together linguists from around the world who share a passion for Mongolic languages — from Khalkha and Buryat to Oirat, Kalmyk, Moghol, and beyond.

Our mission is to foster international collaboration in research, education, and the preservation of these vital languages connecting millions across Central and East Asia.
