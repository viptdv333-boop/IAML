Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the International Association for Mongolic Linguistics ("IAML", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use this website.

Template notice: This policy is a starting point and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before public launch, and adapted to your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR).

1. Information we collect

  • Account data you provide when registering: full name, email address, and password (stored securely, hashed).
  • Profile data you choose to provide: title/position, category, affiliation, biography, photo, and links.
  • Content you submit: comments on publications and any messages you send us.
  • Technical data: limited session/authentication data stored in your browser to keep you signed in.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your member account.
  • To display your profile in the public Participants directory (see below).
  • To send you IAML news and announcements if you opt in.
  • To enable and moderate comments on publications.

3. Public visibility

When you register, the profile information you provide (such as name, title, category, affiliation, and any photo or links you add) is displayed publicly in the Participants directory. Do not include information you do not wish to be public. You may edit or request removal of your profile at any time.

4. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent (given at registration) and our legitimate interest in operating the association. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Service providers

We use Supabase as our backend service provider to host the database, authentication, and storage. Your data is processed on their infrastructure subject to their security and privacy terms.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@iaml.org.

8. Cookies & local storage

We use only essential browser storage required for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising cookies.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@iaml.org.
