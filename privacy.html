Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the International Association for Mongolic Linguistics ("IAML", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use this website.

Template notice: This policy is a starting point and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before public launch, and adapted to your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR).

1. Information we collect

  • Account data you provide when registering: first name, last name, workplace (place of work or study), e-mail address and password (stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form).
  • Profile photo (optional): a picture you upload is resized and stored on our server.
  • Content you submit: questions, replies and reports in the Discuss forum, and messages sent through the contact forms.
  • Mailing-list choices: the Mongolist topics and the frequency (instant, daily or weekly) you select.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, and short-lived server logs (IP address, time, requested page) used for security and abuse prevention.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your member account and to confirm your e-mail address with a one-time code.
  • To show your first name, last name and photo next to your posts in the Discuss forum.
  • To send you the Mongolist e-mails you subscribed to, at the frequency you chose. Every message contains an unsubscribe link.
  • To answer messages sent through the contact forms.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent abuse and moderate the Discuss forum.

3. What is public and what is not

Your first name, last name and photo are shown publicly next to the posts you write in the Discuss forum. The Participants page lists only the officers and board members chosen by the association's administrators; it is not a directory of registered users. Your e-mail address, workplace, password and subscription settings are never shown publicly: your e-mail address and workplace are visible only to the website administrators.

4. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent (given at registration) and our legitimate interest in operating the association. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

5. Service providers

The website and its database run on servers operated for the association. We use Resend (resend.com) to deliver e-mail such as confirmation codes, contact-form notifications and Mongolist messages; it receives the recipient's e-mail address, name and the content of the message for that purpose. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page is opened. We do not use advertising or analytics services.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account yourself on the Account page: your profile, e-mail address, photo and subscriptions are then removed, and the Discuss posts you wrote remain visible as "Deleted user" unless you deleted them individually first. Messages sent through the contact forms are kept for as long as needed to answer them. Technical logs are kept for a short period, and backups may retain removed data for a limited time.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@iaml.org.

8. Cookies & local storage

We use one essential cookie that keeps you signed in. It is not used for tracking and expires after 30 days of inactivity. Your browser's local storage also keeps a cached copy of the site menu, which contains no personal data. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@iaml.org.
